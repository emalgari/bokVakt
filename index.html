Up and running in three minutes

No Python to install. No terminal to open. Just download the app, install it like any other program, and start bookkeeping.

1

Download and install

Grab the installer for your operating system. Double-click it. Done. Bokvakt installs like any normal desktop app, no extra steps.

2

Set up your company

A short wizard asks for your company details, org number, VAT number, and bank info. Save your recovery code somewhere safe and you're done.

3

Start bookkeeping

Add income and expenses as they happen. Create invoices. Bokvakt keeps track of moms, your running balance, and prepares reports for Skatteverket.

Everything you need for Enskild firma

Built for Swedish sole proprietorships. Nothing extra, nothing you don't need, nothing locked behind a paywall.

Swedish invoicing

Professional PDF invoices with sequential numbering, OCR reference, and a proper moms breakdown. Correct every time.

VAT and moms

Output and input VAT, quarterly declarations, and the SKV 4700 boxes mapped for you. Moms is calculated on sales, never on profit.

Gig income

Log weekly or monthly income from Uber, Bolt, or any platform you work with. See totals per platform automatically.

Running balance

See at a glance what you've earned, what you've invoiced, what's still to invoice, and roughly what you owe in tax.

Payroll and salary slips

If you have employees, you can add them, generate Swedish salary slips, and track employer contributions and A-skatt.

Private by design

Your data lives in a local database on your computer. Accounts are secured with Argon2id. Backups are encrypted. Nothing leaves your machine.

Reports for Skatteverket

Momsdeklaration boxes, quarterly summaries, profit and loss, and CSV exports you can use when you file.

Encrypted backups

One-click backups saved as .bokvakt files, encrypted with AES-256. Restore them anywhere, anytime, on any computer.

Multiple accounts

Create separate local accounts with proper passwords. Useful if you share the computer with family or colleagues.

A clean, focused interface

Designed to stay out of your way while you get work done.

Built on the real Swedish rules

Bokvakt follows the Swedish moms rule that a lot of cheap tools get wrong. Moms is calculated on taxable sales, never on your profit.

Every calculation follows ML 1994:200 and maps directly onto the SKV 4700 momsdeklaration. Bokslutsmetoden is supported out of the box.

  • Output VAT on sales at 25, 12, and 6 percent
  • Deductible input VAT on business expenses
  • Box 49 net VAT using the whole kronor rule
  • Owner withdrawals kept out of profit and loss
  • Reverse charge for EU B2B and byggtjanster
  • Sequential invoice numbering with no gaps

Questions people ask

Quick answers about Bokvakt, your data, and Swedish bookkeeping.

Is Bokvakt really free?

Yes. Bokvakt is open source under the MIT license. There's no paid version, no subscription, and no hidden fee. You can read the source code, change it, and share it if you want.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything stays in a local database on your computer. Bokvakt doesn't send anything to any server. You can back up your data as an encrypted .bokvakt file and move it wherever you want.

Does it follow the Skatteverket rules?

Bokvakt follows the Swedish moms rules in ML 1994:200 and maps everything to the SKV 4700 momsdeklaration boxes. It supports bokslutsmetoden too. You still review and file everything yourself in Skatteverket's e-services. Bokvakt helps prepare the numbers, it doesn't file them for you.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes, completely. Bokvakt is local-first. You can use it on a plane, at a cabin, or any time your internet is down. Backups can go to any folder you choose, including one you sync with a service you already use.

Which languages does it support?

Swedish and English. You can switch anytime with one click. Invoice PDFs and salary slips stay in Swedish, since those need to follow Swedish rules.

Can I track Uber, Bolt, and similar income?

Yes. You can record income from any platform such as Uber, Bolt, Foodora, Wolt, or something custom. Weekly or monthly totals are grouped automatically, and you can create invoices for the companies you provided services to.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Bokvakt installs like any other desktop app. The first time you open it, a short wizard walks you through the setup. If you can use a browser, you can use Bokvakt.

Can I contribute or report bugs?

Absolutely. The whole project is on GitHub at github.com/emalgari/bokVakt. You can open an issue if something's broken, or send a pull request if you want to help improve it.
